## Open the starter project

The starter has all the artwork and sounds you need, with empty code areas ready for your blocks.

> [!TASK]
>
> Open the [Lazy Tofu! starter project in Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1438003425/editor/){:target="_blank"} in a new tab.
>
> Keep these instructions open so you can switch between the project and the guide.

> [!TASK]
>
> If you're signed in to Scratch, click **Remix** to make your own copy. Give your project a name.
>
> You can also work without signing in. Use **File → Save to your computer** to download your work, then **File → Load from your computer** when you want to continue.

> [!TASK]
>
> Find the seven sprites in the sprite list: `Tofu`, `tree-left`, `tree-right`, `grass-left`, `grass-right`, `water-ripples` and `objects`.
>
> The Stage already has the river backdrop. Each sprite has its costumes and sounds. You don't need to add or delete any sprites.
>
> Click on **Tofu**, then open the **Code** tab. There are no scripts yet.

This is the starter before you add your code. Pressing its green flag won't start the game yet.
